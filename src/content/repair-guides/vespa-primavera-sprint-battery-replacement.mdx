## Introduction

The battery on a Vespa Primavera and Sprint sits under the seat, directly accessible without tools once the seat is released. Replacement takes around 15 minutes and requires no specialist equipment. This guide applies to all petrol Primavera and Sprint variants produced from 2013 onwards — both 50 cc (carburetor) and 125/150 cc (i-get fuel-injected) engines share the same battery compartment layout and battery type.

A healthy battery is critical on modern Vespas. The fuel injection system, electric starter, instrument panel, and immobiliser all depend on stable 12 V supply. A weak or failing battery commonly presents as intermittent no-start, an instrument panel that resets, or an engine that cranks sluggishly in cold weather.

<Callout type="warn">
  Always disconnect the **negative (–) terminal first** when removing the battery, and reconnect it **last** when fitting the new one. Reversing this order while the key is off risks short-circuiting the electrical system.
</Callout>

## Battery Specification

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Type | 12V Sealed AGM lead-acid |
| Part number | YTX7L-BS |
| Voltage | 12 V |
| Capacity | 6 Ah |
| Cold Cranking Amps | 100 A |
| Terminal polarity | Positive (+) on left side (viewed from front of battery) |
| Compatible OEM ref | Piaggio 584662 / 1D001965 |

Acceptable replacement brands include Yuasa, Piaggio branded PTX7L-BS (OEM), Prima (Scooterworks), and equivalent sealed AGM units. Lithium LiFePO4 drop-in replacements (e.g., PowerTex YTX7L-BS LiFePO4) are also compatible if the charging system is healthy.

<Callout type="tip">
  Buy a sealed, **pre-activated** (maintenance-free) AGM battery whenever possible. These arrive ready to install and do not require acid activation. If you purchase a dry-charged unit, follow the acid-filling instructions before installation.
</Callout>

## Tools & Parts Needed

- Replacement battery: YTX7L-BS (12 V / 6 Ah sealed AGM)
- Phillips screwdriver (for seat release if applicable to your year)
- 8 mm spanner or socket and ratchet
- Clean cloth or latex gloves (battery acid protection, even on sealed units)

## Step-by-Step Procedure

### Step 1 — Open the Seat

Insert the Vespa key and turn it to the seat-release position (or press the remote release button on models so equipped). Lift the seat fully open. The battery is located on the left side of the under-seat compartment, forward of the fuel filler cap.

### Step 2 — Remove the Battery Cover

On most Primavera and Sprint models a small plastic cover or foam pad sits over the battery. Pull it straight up or unclip it — no screws are required on the majority of variants from 2013 onwards.

### Step 3 — Disconnect the Negative Terminal First

Using an 8 mm spanner, loosen the nut on the **black (–) negative lead** and slide the terminal off the battery post. Tuck the lead to one side so it cannot accidentally touch the positive post during the next step.

### Step 4 — Disconnect the Positive Terminal

Loosen the nut on the **red (+) positive lead** and remove it from the battery post. On some models a rubber boot covers the positive terminal — pull this back before loosening the nut.

### Step 5 — Remove the Battery

Lift the battery straight up and out of its tray. Vespa batteries on Primavera/Sprint models are not strapped down — they sit in a moulded plastic tray and are held in place by the cover. The battery weighs approximately 2 kg.

<Callout type="warn">
  Even sealed AGM batteries contain sulphuric acid. Do not tip the battery on its side. Dispose of the old battery at an approved recycling point — most auto parts shops accept used batteries free of charge.
</Callout>

### Step 6 — Inspect the Battery Tray

Before fitting the new battery, wipe out any dust or moisture from the tray. Check the terminal leads for corrosion (white or blue-green powder). If corrosion is present, clean the terminals with a small wire brush and a solution of bicarbonate of soda and water, then rinse and dry thoroughly.

### Step 7 — Install the New Battery

Lower the new battery into the tray with the **positive (+) terminal facing the same direction as the original**. On the Primavera/Sprint, the positive post faces toward the front of the scooter.

### Step 8 — Reconnect Positive Terminal First

Slide the red (+) lead onto the positive post and tighten the 8 mm nut firmly — snug but not over-torqued (do not strip the lead nut). Replace the rubber boot if present.

### Step 9 — Reconnect the Negative Terminal

Slide the black (–) lead onto the negative post and tighten the nut.

### Step 10 — Replace the Cover and Close the Seat

Refit the plastic battery cover or foam pad. Close the seat and confirm it latches correctly.

### Step 11 — Start the Scooter and Verify

Turn the key to the ON position. The instrument panel should illuminate normally. Start the engine and confirm normal idle. On i-get (injected) models the panel may perform a self-check sequence — this is normal after a battery disconnection.

## Post-Replacement Notes

After fitting a new battery on an injected Primavera or Sprint, the throttle body idle may need a short adaptation run. Ride the scooter for 10–15 minutes with a mix of idle, low-speed, and highway speeds to allow the ECU to re-learn the idle baseline.

If the scooter is stored for more than 3–4 weeks at a time, connect a 12 V smart trickle charger (0.8–1.5 A) to maintain battery charge. Allowing an AGM battery to fully discharge more than a few times will permanently reduce its capacity and lifespan.

Expected battery service life under normal conditions is 3–5 years.
