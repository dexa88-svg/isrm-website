The **Piaggio Zip 50 4T** is one of the most popular urban 50cc scooters in Europe, produced continuously from 1992 to the present day in multiple generations. The 4T designation refers to the 4-stroke engine, which replaced the earlier 2-stroke variants in most markets following tightening emissions regulations. The ZIP is particularly common in the Netherlands, Belgium, France, and Italy, where it frequently appears as a _bromfiets_ or _cyclomoteur_ in its 25 km/h restricted form, as well as in full 45 km/h form.

This page covers the 4T carburetor variants produced from approximately 2001 to the present. Euro 4/5 fuel-injected versions of the ZIP use the same frame but a different engine management system — see the diagnostics section for EFI fault codes.

## Carburetor — Dell'Orto PHBN 12 HS

The Piaggio ZIP 50 4T uses a **Dell'Orto PHBN 12 HS** slide carburetor. The 12 refers to the bore diameter in mm. This is a simple, reliable unit that rarely requires adjustment in normal use. Key settings:

<table><tbody><tr><th>Setting</th><th>Specification</th></tr><tr><td>Main jet</td><td>56 (standard, sea level)</td></tr><tr><td>Idle jet (pilot jet)</td><td>35</td></tr><tr><td>Needle position</td><td>3rd clip from top (middle position, standard)</td></tr><tr><td>Idle speed</td><td>1,800–2,000 RPM (warm engine, rear wheel off ground)</td></tr><tr><td>Float height</td><td>12–13 mm (measured from carb body mating face to float)</td></tr></tbody></table>

### Idle Speed Adjustment Procedure

1.  Warm the engine to full operating temperature — at least 10 minutes of riding or idling.
2.  Locate the idle speed screw on the carburettor body — a flat-head screw with a spring, accessible from the right side of the engine.
3.  With the rear wheel off the ground, turn the screw clockwise to increase idle speed, anti-clockwise to decrease.
4.  Set idle to 1,800–2,000 RPM. The rear wheel must not spin at idle — if it does, decrease idle speed.
5.  Check that idle drops cleanly after releasing the throttle from cruising speed. A stumble or stall indicates a lean idle mixture — turn the mixture screw anti-clockwise by ¼ turns until idle is smooth.

### Air/Fuel Mixture Screw

The mixture screw is located on the engine-side of the carburettor body, often covered by a pressed-in cap to prevent tampering. Factory setting is typically 1.5–2.0 turns out from lightly seated. To adjust: turn in (clockwise) to lean the idle mixture; turn out (anti-clockwise) to richen. Make ¼-turn increments and re-check idle quality after each adjustment.

## Common Problems & Solutions

<table><tbody><tr><th>Symptom</th><th>Most Likely Cause</th><th>Fix</th></tr><tr><td>Hard cold start</td><td>Choke cable stuck or choke not seating; old fuel</td><td>Free choke cable; drain and refill fuel tank with fresh 95 RON</td></tr><tr><td>Rough idle or stalling</td><td>Blocked idle/pilot jet; air leak at intake manifold</td><td>Clean idle jet with carb cleaner; check manifold O-ring</td></tr><tr><td>Loss of top speed</td><td>Worn drive belt; worn variator rollers; restricted airbox</td><td>Replace belt and/or rollers; clean air filter</td></tr><tr><td>Excessive fuel consumption</td><td>Float stuck open (carb flooding); rich mixture</td><td>Inspect and clean float valve; adjust mixture screw</td></tr><tr><td>Engine overheating</td><td>Low oil level; blocked cooling fins; lean mixture</td><td>Check and top up oil; clean cylinder fin area; check jetting</td></tr><tr><td>Blue-white exhaust smoke</td><td>Oil consumption — worn piston rings or valve stem seals</td><td>Compression test; consider top end inspection</td></tr><tr><td>No electric start</td><td>Weak battery; blown fuse; failed starter relay</td><td>Charge/replace battery; check 15A main fuse; test starter relay</td></tr></tbody></table>

## Identification & Model History

The Piaggio ZIP 50 4T has gone through several generations. Key identifiers:

-   **ZIP 50 4T (2001–2008):** ZAPC25 frame code. Rounded body style. Dell'Orto PHBN carburettor. Single front disc or drum depending on market. Covered by the Haynes manual (publication 4837).
-   **ZIP 50 4T (2008–2013, Euro 2):** ZAPC2510 frame code. Updated front fairing, same powertrain. Carburettor.
-   **ZIP 50 4T (2013–2017, Euro 3):** Updated suspension, catalytic converter, retained carburettor on some markets.
-   **ZIP 50 4T (2018–present, Euro 4/5):** Fuel injection replaces carburettor. Electronic throttle. Different ECU-based diagnostics. Refer to EFI fault code guide for diagnostics.

The Vehicle Identification Number (VIN/VIN plate) is located on the steering head (headset) and also stamped into the frame under the seat. The VIN encodes the model year, assembly plant, and engine variant.

## Parts Sourcing

OEM parts are available from authorised Piaggio dealers and online from [EasyParts](https://www.easyparts.com/models/Piaggio-m420), [CMS NL](https://www.cmsnl.com/), and [SIP Scootershop](https://www.sip-scootershop.com/). Aftermarket parts (drive belts, rollers, filters, spark plugs) are widely available and often interchangeable with other Piaggio 50cc 4T platforms including the Vespa ET2 4T, Piaggio Liberty 50 4T, and Gilera Runner 50 4T, which share the same engine family.
